Rules of the game

On the player's side, there are five characters (A, B, C, D, and E). They stand in a row, where A is to the left of B, B is to the left of C, C is to the left of D, and D is to the left of E, and E is considered to be to the left of A.

At the beginning of each round, each character is randomly assigned a stick and a bucket.
There are five sticks and five buckets, and each character can only have one stick and one bucket.
The sticks and buckets are in five different colors: red, blue, green, yellow, and purple.

The sticks and buckets are passed between the characters based on their actions.
When a character attacks, their stick is passed to the first character to their right, and their bucket is passed to the second character to their left;
when a character defends, their stick is passed to the second character to their right, and their bucket is passed to the first character to their left.

The player's side is only able to deal damage to the enemy when the colors of the sticks and the buckets match for all five characters, and the characters are allowed 31 actions in total for each round to match the colors.
